## Rachel Murphy

Rachel Murphy was a PhD candidate and worked, among other topics, on corpora and NLP models for drug-induced acute kidney injury (AKI). Rachel was also a member of the [Pharmacoinformatics Lab](https://www.pharmacoinformaticslab.nl) and was co-supervised with [dr. Joanna Klopotowska](https://www.amsterdamumc.org/en/research/researchers/joanna-klopotowska.htm).
