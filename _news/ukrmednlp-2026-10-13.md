The project [UkrMedNLP: Building Responsible Medical NLP Resources for a Low-Resource Language](https://www.nwo.nl/en/projects/dziti36026) was awarded €30,000 funding by NWO. UkrMedNLP will complement CaRe-NLP by creating biomedical resources in Ukrainian, enabling multilingual development and evaluation of NLP methods for healthcare.
